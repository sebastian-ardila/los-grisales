## Enjoy the 2026 Harvest Festival with the aroma of coffee

The Fiestas de la Cosecha (Harvest Festival) 2026 is one of the most special times to visit Pereira. During this season, the city fills up with cultural activities, music, food and experiences that celebrate the identity of our region.

If you're wondering what to do in Pereira at this time of year, add an experience around specialty coffee to your itinerary — one of the greatest sources of pride in the Coffee Region.

At Café Los Grisales you can enjoy drinks made with coffee grown on our own farm, discover new ways to brew and taste coffee, share a menu designed to pair with every cup and find out why Colombian coffee is recognised all over the world.

And if you'd like an even more complete experience, we invite you to our Coffee Tour, where you'll walk through the plantations, follow the coffee process from seed to cup and enjoy an authentic journey through our coffee culture.

**📍 Visit us at:**

- **CC Unicentro Pereira** — Level B, food court.
- **CC Pereira Plaza** — Unit 209.
