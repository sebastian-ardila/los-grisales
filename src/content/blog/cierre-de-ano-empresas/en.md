## Your company celebration, in the heart of the Coffee Region

Looking for a different way to celebrate your team's achievements and say goodbye to the year? At Café Los Grisales we invite you to swap the usual venues for an experience that connects you with nature and coffee culture.

At our **Finca Vista Hermosa, in Alto del Toro, Dosquebradas**, you and your coworkers can share a day that combines food, landscapes and a tour to discover coffee from its origin.

For this season we have prepared a special year-end experience for corporate groups of 10 people or more, with two options to choose from:

### Morning experience

- Breakfast.
- Coffee tour of the farm.
- Coffee natilla and buñuelo to share.

### Afternoon experience

- Lunch.
- Coffee tour of the farm.
- Coffee natilla and buñuelo to share.
