## This time, your coffee helps too

After what happened in Pereira on August 10 and the hard moments we have lived through as a team, we wanted to turn this situation into an opportunity to support and help our community.

That is why we created **“A cup that helps”**, a campaign that brings together two things that represent us: our coffee and the people who are part of our story.

During the campaign, for every pound of Café Los Grisales bought at our Unicentro shop, you will receive a complimentary americano.

The value of that americano will be donated to rebuild the home of a relative of one of our team members.

So every time you choose to take our coffee home, you are also contributing to a cause we want to support.

## How can you take part?

1. Visit us at Unicentro Pereira, Level B, food court.
2. Buy a pound of Café Los Grisales.
3. Receive a complimentary americano.
4. The value of that americano goes to the donation.

One purchase may seem small, but when many people join in, it can turn into something much bigger.

Thank you for continuing to choose Café Los Grisales, for visiting us and for helping us move forward. This time, we want every cup to also be a way of sharing.

📍 **Café Los Grisales** — CC Unicentro Pereira, Level B, food court.
