## A pause at Pereira Plaza, but the story goes on

On August 10, the earthquake that hit Pereira also reached our coffee shop at Pereira Plaza, leaving the space with damage that forces us to close it temporarily.

It has been a hard moment for our brand, but also a chance to remember that Café Los Grisales is much more than a place: it is a community of people who believe in coffee, in the countryside and in the projects born out of love for our land.

While we work to recover our Pereira Plaza shop, we want to keep sharing with you what we are most passionate about.

## You can find us at

**CC Unicentro Pereira** – Level B, food court.

There we will keep brewing our specialty coffee, our drinks, our cocktails and the experience that defines Café Los Grisales.

And if you want to live coffee from seed to cup, we also invite you to join our **Coffee Tour**, an experience at our farm where you will discover the coffee process, the coffee culture and the landscapes of Colombia's Coffee Region.

Thank you for continuing to support Café Los Grisales. Every visit, every cup of coffee and every shared experience helps us keep building this story. We hope to be back at Pereira Plaza very soon; in the meantime, we would love to welcome you at Unicentro and at our farm.
