## Keep travelling. Keep discovering. Keep supporting our region.

Our region still has a lot to discover: its coffee landscapes, its food, its hotels, restaurants, cultural experiences and all those small businesses that are part of the life of our cities.

So if you are planning your next trip through Colombia or you want to get to know the Coffee Region, we invite you to keep Pereira and Dosquebradas on your itinerary.

## Every visit counts

When you choose to stay at a hotel in the region, eat at a local restaurant, visit a landmark, buy products from our land or live an experience such as a Coffee Tour, you are helping many people and businesses keep going.

Because travelling is also a way of supporting, connecting and helping the destinations we visit to keep growing.

Pereira and Dosquebradas still have stories to tell, flavours to discover and experiences to live.

When you plan your next trip through Colombia, consider our region. We will be waiting for you.

## Discover the region

- **Café Los Grisales** – CC Unicentro Pereira, Level B, food court.
- **Coffee Tour – Finca Vista Hermosa** – Alto del Toro, Dosquebradas, Risaralda.
